Executive Summary
Executive Summary

ATLAS is a fully autonomous, AI-powered paper trading agent designed for the NSE (National Stock Exchange) of India. It operates a complete end-to-end trading pipeline — from data ingestion and technical analysis, through intelligent signal scoring and trade validation, to real-time monitoring and adaptive learning — all without human intervention during market hours.

The system scans the entire NSE equity universe of 2,357+ stocks every 5 minutes during market hours using a proprietary two-stage scanning architecture, confirms setups across 5m, 15m, 1h, and 1d market structure, incorporates official exchange announcements and financial news, validates trades against strict risk rules, and executes paper trades with defined targets and stop-losses. After each trading session, a learning agent analyzes closed trades and autonomously updates the strategy — creating a continuously improving system.

Core Modules
📥 Data Fetcher data/fetcher.py
  • Fetches 5m, 15m, 1h, and 1d OHLCV candles via Yahoo Finance
  • Priority cadence: 5m/15m every cycle, 1h every 30 min, 1d daily at 09:00 IST
  • Auto-backfills missing historical candle data on first run
  • Stores data in local SQLite — zero repeated API calls
  • Retry logic with exponential backoff for network failures
🌐 Universe Fetcher data/universe_fetcher.py
  • Downloads the full NSE equity + F&O stock list weekly
  • Classifies stocks by market cap (Large / Mid / Small cap)
  • Maintains avg_daily_volume for Stage 1 volume ratio
  • Persists 2,357+ symbols in stock_universe table
📐 Indicator Engine analysis/indicator_engine.py
  • RSI (14) — Momentum oscillator for overbought/oversold
  • MACD (12,26,9) — Trend direction & crossovers
  • EMA 20 & 50 — Short & medium trend tracking
  • VWAP — Volume-weighted price benchmark
  • Confluence: 5m / 15m / 1h / 1d trend agreement
  • Volume Ratio — Today volume vs 10-day average
  • Requires 250 warmup candles per symbol for accuracy
🔍 Stage 1 Scanner analysis/stage1_scanner.py
  • Scans all 2,357 NSE stocks in 2.4 seconds
  • Price change threshold: >0.5% (configurable)
  • Volume surge threshold: >1.5× 10-day average
  • Always includes all 210 F&O stocks (max liquidity)
  • Always flags stocks with open positions
  • Fetches NSE Bhav Copy CSV (single download)
🎯 Stage 2 Scanner analysis/stage2_scanner.py
  • Accepts flagged symbols from Stage 1 only
  • Runs full signal scoring via signal_engine
  • Detects market condition: TRENDING / SIDEWAYS / VOLATILE
  • Requires at least 2 of 3 main timeframes to agree
  • Filters by min_score_to_trade threshold (default: 60)
  • Sorts candidates by score descending
  • Uses confluence score as a final signal multiplier
  • Feeds top 5 candidates into NewsAPI enrichment
📈 Signal Engine analysis/signal_engine.py
  • Configurable 4-indicator weighted scoring (0–100)
  • RSI Score: Tiered thresholds (max 25 pts)
  • MACD Score: Crossover (25pts) + histogram (15pts)
  • Volume Score: Ratio tiers (max 25 pts at 2.5×)
  • VWAP Score: Position & distance scoring (max 25 pts)
  • Boosts: confluence multiplier + announcement signal boost
  • Reads config from signal_config.json (live-editable)
📰 News Intelligence data/news_fetcher.py
  • Reads official NSE/BSE corporate announcements
  • Fetches free financial RSS feeds every 15 minutes
  • Runs Google News only for Stage 1 flagged stocks
  • Runs NewsAPI only for top 5 Stage 2 candidates
  • Tracks NewsAPI daily usage with 90-call safety guard
✅ Trade Validator agents/trade_validator.py
  • Check 1: Market hours guard (09:15–15:15 IST)
  • Check 2: Minimum confidence score threshold
  • Check 3: Maximum open trades limit
  • Check 4: No duplicate trades (same symbol)
  • Check 5: Capital sufficiency check
  • Check 6: Market condition filter
  • Check 7: Data freshness validation
📏 Risk Manager agents/risk_manager.py
  • Max capital per trade: 20% of portfolio
  • Stop-loss distance: 0.75% from entry price
  • Target: +1.5% for BUY, -1.5% for SELL (2:1 R/R)
  • Position size from risk_per_trade_pct
  • Reads live balance from paper_account table

Orchestrator & Scheduler

The Orchestrator is the heartbeat of ATLAS. Built on APScheduler, it runs a cron-triggered cycle every 5 minutes during NSE market hours (09:15–15:30 IST, Mon–Fri). It sequences market data, indicators, announcements, news, scanning, execution, monitoring, and learning while managing failure counters, session statistics, graceful shutdown, and status.json health reporting.

🏃 Scheduler Specs
  • Engine: APScheduler BlockingScheduler
  • Main trigger: CronTrigger every 5 min
  • 1h candles + announcements every 30 min
  • RSS/NewsAPI intelligence every 15 min
  • Daily candles at 09:00; pre-market announcements at 09:10
  • Failure limit: 3 consecutive → stage disabled

Failure Resilience & Health Monitoring

⚠️ Stage Failure Tracking
Each pipeline stage has an independent failure counter. If a stage fails 3 consecutive times, it is automatically disabled for the session to prevent cascading errors. Tracked stages include fetcher, hourly_candles, daily_candles, announcements, news, indicator_engine, stage1_scanner, stage2_scanner, trade_execution, and trade_monitoring.
✅ Graceful Shutdown
  • On SIGINT/SIGTERM waits for active cycle (up to 240s)
  • Writes status: stopped to status.json
  • Logs full session summary (opened/closed trades)
  • Safe to Ctrl+C at any time without data corruption

Risk, Compliance & Closing
Risk & Compliance

⚠️ Technical Risks

Data Dependency Risk
Stage 2 requires recent candle data. If Yahoo Finance API is unavailable, Stage 2 is skipped — Stage 1 still runs. 3-day backfill + retry logic handles failures.
NSE API Rate Limits
Stage 1 uses NSE Bhav Copy (single daily CSV). Session cookies managed automatically. Retry adapter with backoff handles 429/5xx.
NewsAPI Quota
NewsAPI free tier is protected by a 90-call/day guard and is used only for top 5 Stage 2 candidates. RSS, Google News, and exchange announcements continue if quota is exhausted.
Database Locking
SQLite WAL mode for concurrent readers. Threading locks on cycle prevent concurrent writes. 325 MB+ stable, WAL journal active.

🛡️ Built-in Safeguards

  • Paper-only by default — No real money at risk
  • Market hours guard — 09:15–15:15 IST only
  • Max position limit — Max 3 concurrent trades
  • Capital cap — Max 20% of balance per trade
  • Stop-loss mandatory — 0.75% on every trade
  • EOD forced close — All positions by 15:30 IST
  • Score threshold — Min 60/100 to trade
  • Duplicate prevention — No two same-symbol trades
  • News quota guard — NewsAPI stops at 90 calls/day
  • 3-strike disable — Failing stages auto-disabled
  • Audit trail — Every signal decision logged
SEBI / Regulatory: ATLAS is a paper trading system only. No real trades, no real money, no SEBI algo registration required. Market and disclosure data comes from public NSE/BSE sources, Yahoo Finance, RSS feeds, and optional NewsAPI enrichment.
Data Privacy: No user personal data collected. All data local — no cloud uploads, no third-party data sharing.
